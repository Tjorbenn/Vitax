Vitax
Visualizing the NCBI Taxonomy

Navigate the NCBI Taxonomy with Ease

Vitax helps researchers and students visualize and explore complex taxonomic relationships on an interactive canvas.

Unlock new insights for biological research by transforming hierarchical taxonomy data into an intuitive visual experience.

Features

1/5

NCBI Querying & Lineage Resolution

Query scientific or common names to fetch full NCBI taxonomy lineage paths, resolving ancestral and descendant taxonomy nodes automatically.

Interactive Layouts

Compare representations using node-link Trees, force-directed Graphs and nested Circle Packs.

Complex Taxonomic Relationships

Investigate and visualize complex relationships: View Descendant Lineages, discover Taxonomic Neighbors, or find the Most Recent Common Ancestor (MRCA).

Genomic Data

Explore and filter genomes associated with taxa in the NCBI database and export the accessions or assemblies directly for downstream analysis.

Literature & External Metadata

Discover related publications from Semantic Scholar, or explore links to external sources.
